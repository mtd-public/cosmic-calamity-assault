1997. The Committee made a deal. The visitors broke it.

Special Agent Eli Marsh spent six years in a basement office proving the government knew. He was right. Now he's escaping Area 51 with a stolen dossier and a hard drive that talks back: IRIS, an AI grown from alien hardware, who draws his waypoints, explains what's happening, and knows where the walls are hollow.

Four maps

Area 51 under siege, from its desert yard and warehouse down to the labs; a seedy late-90s Washington at night; a crash site in the Cascades; the mothership.

Call of Duty handling

Hip-fire from the right, aim down sights, dual-wield anything (shotguns too), cook grenades, stick Predator-style alien detonators.

Move like it's 2007

Dolphin-dive while you shoot, clamber up ledges and window sills, sprint. It's still Doom underneath.

Custom characters

Greys, possessed Thralls, Hybrids, Stalkers, Probes and Overseers. Every sprite is pre-rendered from 3D in 8 rotations.

People worth saving

Scientists flee and cower. Revive downed guards and they fight beside you.

Hack and hold

Download, upload, defend: terminals take a minute or more while the waves come.

How to play

  1. Install GZDoom 4.14 or its community fork UZDoom (Windows, macOS, Linux).
  2. Get a base game: Freedoom: Phase 2 (freedoom2.wad, free) or your own DOOM II (doom2.wad).
  3. Download cosmic-calamity-assault.pk3.
  4. Drag the .pk3 onto GZDoom, or run:
    gzdoom -iwad freedoom2.wad -file cosmic-calamity-assault.pk3
  5. First run: open Options → Controller Layout (Xbox) or Keyboard & Mouse Layout and choose Apply.

A browser version isn't possible: GZDoom has no web build. The page links the previous three.js prototype, which does run in the browser.

Everything is generated by code in the repository: sprites and textures are rendered from procedural three.js models, sounds are synthesised, music is generated MIDI, and the maps are compiled from ASCII with a completability check. Marsh, IRIS, Kade and the Custodian are original characters. Requires GZDoom (GPL) with Freedoom (BSD) or DOOM II.
