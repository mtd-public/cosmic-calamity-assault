; MAP04 "The Loop" - downtown Chicago, 1997
; Flow: State Street under the Chicago Theatre marquee (start) -> Lake Street under the L -> Daley
; Plaza (the Picasso) -> stairs up to the State/Lake platform -> fight along the L (Lake, Wabash) ->
; the skybridge from the Wabash L into the MERCHANT TOWER's 2nd floor (or walk in through the lobby
; on Randolph) -> the stair core: lobby 0, floors at 192 / 384 / 576 (the 3rd floor is burning),
; roof 768; the express elevator runs lobby <-> 4th floor. A 20 s comms terminal on the 4th floor
; (IRIS traces the relay) completes obj 1 ->
; the roof: the harvest relay grown into the antenna, 60 s HackTerminal holdout (obj 2), which drops
; the barrier to the window-washer platform -> ride it down the outside of the tower to the alley ->
; the State/Lake station house on Lake Street -> the ExitGate (objectives 1+2) opens the door to the
; exit platform -> walk into the waiting L train (exit, obj 3).
; Civilians: 14 office workers across the floors, most of them near aliens; SafeZones at the lobby
; doors, the stairwell exit in the lobby and the roof stair head.
; Tower construction: one footprint with four stacked 3D floors (176..192, 368..384, 560..576,
; 752..768). Walls that exist on one floor only are regions whose slabs merge through that floor
; ('w' floor 2, 'x' floor 3, 'y' floor 4, 'd' a lobby desk). Core walls are blocks up to the roof.
; Window bays are 2-cell-deep window sectors whose sill/lintel bracket one floor.
; Secrets: the back room south of Randolph (assault rifle), the CTA substation.
